🔎 Wi-Fi Safety Check

Answer five questions. This is an educational checklist, not a scan of your network.

1. Did you confirm the exact Wi-Fi network name with the venue?

2. Is your device up to date?

3. Are you avoiding unexpected login pages and suspicious links?

4. Is multifactor authentication enabled on important accounts?

5. Will you use cellular data or another trusted connection for especially sensitive tasks?

🎓 Learn Public Wi-Fi Safety

📶 Fake hotspots

An attacker may imitate a legitimate network name. Ask staff to confirm the network before joining.

🔐 Secure browsing

HTTPS encrypts traffic to a website, but a padlock does not prove a site is trustworthy. Check the full address.

📱 Protect accounts

Enable MFA, update devices, disable auto-join, and avoid entering credentials on unexpected captive portals.

🛡️ VPN basics

A trusted VPN can add protection on untrusted networks, but cannot stop phishing or make unsafe sites safe.
